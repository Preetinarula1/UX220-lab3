Being Vegetarian

The best thing I have read lately is Being Vegetarian by Rebecca Hammons. It is not a cookbook. Instead, it explains why people choose to be vegetarian or vegan and how to live that way in a world where most people eat meat.

The book talks about the good reasons to be vegetarian, like better health, helping the environment, and being kind to animals. It also shows the harm that meat production can cause to people, animals, and our planet. I liked that the facts are backed by scientific research.

The book also gives helpful tips, such as how to eat healthy, how to spot hidden animal ingredients in food, and how to handle questions from family and friends. It was easy to read and gave me clear answers to the question, "Why are you vegetarian?"
